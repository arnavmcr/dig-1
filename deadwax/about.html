Dead Wax

Liner notes

The dead wax is the area between the last groove and the label — the part of a record that holds no music, just the hand-etched marks left by the cutting engineer. A reference number. A secret message. Proof that a human made this.

This is a personal archive. Every record here was ripped, tagged, and listened to. Nothing is here by algorithm. The genres are rooms in a store I've been building in my head for years — each one with its own light and temperature.

Use the floor to browse. Use the bag to collect. Copy the list and take it somewhere else. That's all this needs to do.
